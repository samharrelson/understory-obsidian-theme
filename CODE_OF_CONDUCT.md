# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Understory a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

This project values curiosity, generosity, thoughtful disagreement, and attention to the effects our words and actions have on others.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a healthy community include:

- being respectful and considerate;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback in good faith;
- acknowledging differing perspectives and levels of experience;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, design, and behavior rather than people;
- accepting responsibility and making reasonable efforts to repair harm when mistakes happen;
- helping keep the project useful, calm, and collaborative.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- personal attacks, insults, or sustained hostility;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberate disruption of project spaces;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or collaborative setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this code of conduct, please report it privately to:

**Sam Harrelson**  
sam@samharrelson.com

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Information will be shared only as needed to understand and respond to the situation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

The goal of enforcement is to protect the health of the community, not to create unnecessary punishment.
